Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
ApplyRank is a student portfolio project, not a company. It is written in plain words on purpose. Please do not enter anything you are not comfortable sharing, and never use a password you use elsewhere.

What we store

  • Account: your name, e-mail and a scrambled (hashed) password. We can never read your password.
  • Profile and activity: the profile details you add, skill-test answers and scores, jobs you save or apply to, and notifications.
  • Resume file (Resume Analyzer): if you upload a resume on the dashboard, the file and its analysis result are stored so you can see them again.
  • AI Lab checks: only small facts, such as which tool you used, when, and whether the AI part ran (this counts your hourly limit). Your pasted text and files are not stored.
  • Security log: events like login, failed login, password reset, data download and account deletion, with your e-mail, the time and your IP address. It helps to catch abuse. These entries can stay after an account is deleted.
  • In your browser: a login token and a few settings are kept in your browser storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

The AI Lab and Google Gemini

The AI Lab first checks your text with built-in rules, on our server. The AI part is optional and only runs if you tick the consent box. If you do, the text you pasted (or the text read from your file) is sent to Google's Gemini API to get friendly advice. Before sending, the page hides e-mail addresses, phone numbers and links in a resume. Names and other details you typed are not hidden, so remove them yourself if you wish. On Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products. If you do not want that, leave the AI box unticked: the rule-based result still works.

Who can see your data

  • Recruiters on ApplyRank see your full e-mail only after they shortlist you.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • Service providers that keep the site running (hosting, database, optional e-mail sending, Google Fonts for the font, and Google Gemini only if you agree above) process data on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile page → Privacy & data → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile page → Delete my account. Your account, profile, test results, applications, saved jobs, notifications and uploaded resume files are deleted for good.
  • Log out everywhere: Profile page → Account & security.

Security

Passwords are hashed, logins use signed tokens that stop working after logout or a password reset, the API limits how fast one address can call it, and the secrets (database password, AI key, token secret) are kept outside the code. No system is perfectly safe, so please use a strong, unique password.
