How to verify any offer yourself

1. Find the company's official website on your own (do not use the link in the message) and look for the job on its careers page.
2. The email should end with the company's own domain, not gmail.com or yahoo.com.
3. Search the company name with the word "scam" or "fake offer".
4. Never pay money, never share an OTP, and never install remote-control apps.
